FAQ

Good questions

Is the mint really free?

Yes. Each Muse is free to mint. You only pay the network gas fee on Robinhood Chain, which is usually a few cents.

Which chain is it on?

Robinhood Chain, chain ID 4663. You pay gas in ETH on that chain. No wallet connection is needed to join the waitlist.

How many Muses are there?

3,333 unique Muses. No two are the same.

What is a blind reveal?

The artwork stays hidden while the mint runs. Once all 3,333 are minted, every Muse is revealed at once.

I hold a Foxora. What do I get?

Foxora holders get in first with guaranteed access, then the waitlist opens. Paste your address in the form above and we check it on-chain.

When is the mint?

The date is not set yet. Join the waitlist and follow @musenftxyz for the announcement.
